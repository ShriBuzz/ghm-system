# Call with NEEK, 13 Nov 2026: scope and open questions

Prepared on 12 Nov 2026 for the call with NEEK about the clarifications, RFQ NBI/GHM/RFQ/01.

We want three things agreed by the end of the call:

- what this contract delivers at go-live on 27 Nov;
- which of NEEK's new requirements wait for a later phase, priced separately;
- answers to the questions in section 4.

NEEK's WhatsApp answers and the brief on its GHM Policy and SOP updates arrived on 12 Nov. They settled most of our questions. Everything that answers a question we asked is now built, together with the brief's public page text.

## 2. New requirements outside the RFQ

The brief adds requirements that RFQ NBI/GHM/RFQ/01 section 3 does not contain. We checked each one against the RFQ text. We cannot build them within this contract, for two reasons.

- **The deadline.** The test version is due on 18 Nov. UAT runs from 20 to 25 Nov, training is on 25 Nov and go-live is on 27 Nov. Together these items need about two more weeks of development and testing, which cannot fit before go-live without putting the RFQ's own dates at risk.
- **The cost.** The contract is a fixed price for the RFQ's requirements, as set out in our proposal. Under the proposal, work beyond the RFQ is a change request. It is quoted and agreed separately before it starts.

Our proposal: we deliver the RFQ scope, with NEEK's answers, on 27 Nov as planned. NEEK tells us which of the items below it wants. We then quote them as a second phase, to start after go-live.

None of these items blocks go-live. Where an item overlaps an RFQ requirement, the RFQ part is already built, as the last column shows.

## 4. Questions still open

### Needed before go-live on 27 Nov

Staging is planned for 16 to 18 Nov, so the first three are the most urgent.

1. Hosting (our question 1): which host, and access for the staging and production servers.
2. Email sending (our question 3): the mail account or SMTP service for alerts and acknowledgements.
3. Access logs (our question 4): does the host keep web-server logs with visitors' IP addresses, and can they be shortened or turned off? The system itself stores no IP addresses.
4. C12: the hotline number and the grievance email address for "Other ways to raise a grievance".
5. The company's full name and logo for the public pages and emails. The public site header still reads "Grievance handling".
6. The first Administrator: the Senior Manager's work email for the account at go-live. The IT contact gets server access only, with no panel login, as the brief says.
7. C6, who may export: the brief's section 8 says "Administrator only", while the answers say focal persons export what they can see. Today each role exports the cases it can see, and only the Administrator can include names and contacts.
8. Our question 16: how complainants who use the drop boxes or the phone follow their case, and the language and wording of the printed slip.
9. Our question 9: we keep "anonymous means no contact details", so an anonymous complainant gives no email. Please confirm.
10. A Nepali reviewer from NEEK to read all the Nepali text before UAT. That includes the BS month names, the form's messages, our Nepali heading for "Other ways", our advice on the received page and the new "About you" hint.

### Needed during UAT, 20 to 25 Nov

1. C22 in detail: with an exact total, a "<5" can sometimes be worked out. In testing, a restricted-case officer saw "2 cases received" next to a single "<5". Should the total be hidden or rounded as well?
2. C24: may staff enter the incident date in Bikram Sambat when they enter an offline case? We suggest yes; it is a small change.
3. Export column names: the export now has "Received (AD)", "Received (BS)", "Closed (AD)" and "Closed (BS)". If NEEK's UNDP reporting sheet reads columns by name, it needs the new names.
4. Submissions from one network: the form accepts 10 grievances an hour from one internet connection, to stop automated spam. How many workers share one connection, for example the factory Wi-Fi? We can raise the limit before the QR posters go up.
5. The activity log: entries are kept with no end date. How long should they be kept?
6. The user guide's "Use it when" line for each status is our suggested wording. Please confirm it or correct it.
7. Dates on the Nepali status page: the complainant's status page shows the received date in AD, such as "12 नोभेम्बर 2026", although the Nepali form asks for a BS date. Should it show BS, as the staff screens do?

### Only for the quote, if NEEK wants the new items

1. C10: the Policy (section 10, for the feedback questions) and the SOP (sections 6, 7.1, 8.2 and 9), which the brief cites.
2. C13: is the structured feedback form on the status page wanted? The dashboard's feedback figures need it.
3. C14: a Nepali staff panel later?
4. C16: a reopened case's target: the category's target again, or 15 working days for an appeal? Does an earlier extension carry over?
5. C17: is each extension after the first also limited to 15 working days?
6. C18: does the closure rule (a feedback note, or two failed contacts) also apply to Referred / Out of scope cases?
7. C23: dashboard quarters by calendar year (AD) or by the Nepali fiscal year?
8. C25: should the case list flag cases not assessed within 7 working days?
9. C26: "date of each status change" in the export: one column per status, with the last date the case entered it?
10. Retention: a delete action for cases closed more than 5 years ago, or the rule written into the handover documents for now? No case can qualify before 2031.
