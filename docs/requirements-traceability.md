# Requirements traceability

One row per RFQ requirement (wording in `docs/rfq-requirements.md`), plus the plan's implied rows X1 and X2 and the company's updates U1 to U16: where each lives in the code, which tests prove it, and its status. Last updated 2026-11-12: the company's answers and its Policy and SOP updates arrived. Rows U1 to U16 are added, and the RFQ rows they change say so in their notes. They are work package WP4. Steps 1 (the company's lists and labels, U8), 2 (the count restarting on reopening, U3, and holidays entered by focal persons) 3 (Bikram Sambat dates on the Nepali form, U9), 10 (Bikram Sambat dates and Nepali names on the staff screens, U9 and U16), 12 (both calendars in the export, U9), 4 (the company's text on the public pages, U10), 7 (the company's alert rules, U6) and 5 (a reason for removing a restriction, U5) and 11 ("<5" for small groups in the summary, U11) are built; the rest is not yet.

Every Pest test is grouped by its ID and every Playwright spec tagged with it, so one requirement can be checked on its own:

```sh
php artisan test --group=A5
npx playwright test --grep @A5
```

Status counts the whole requirement, not the part one work package built:

- **Not started**: nothing built yet; schema columns alone do not count.
- **In progress**: part is built and tested, the rest is named in the notes.
- **Tested**: everything the RFQ asks is built, with passing Pest (and Playwright, where a browser can see it) checks.
- **UAT passed**: signed off by NBI or the company. Nothing is at this stage before the 18 Nov test version.
